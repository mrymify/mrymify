How It All Started

From a Classroom Idea to 400+ Smiles

A quick look at how our student-led startup blossomed over the past 6 months.

The Spark • 2nd Semester

A University Course Idea

During our 2nd semester, we needed an original business concept for one of our university courses. We decided to create a few handcrafted crochet samples. To our joy, friends and professors were mesmerized by the quality, and we received 4 to 5 orders on the spot! That immediate enthusiasm showed us we had something special.

Official Launch • April

Taking the Leap into Mrymify

Encouraged by that initial spark, we officially registered and launched our startup in April. Between university lectures, assignments, and study sessions, we dedicated our evenings to perfecting yarn palettes, standardizing stitch density, and building our online presence.

The 6-Day Pop-Up Stall

Finals Week & Campus Excitement

One of our most memorable milestones was hosting an artisan stall at our university for 6 full days. Balancing final exams, semester projects, and high customer demand was intense, but the overwhelming love from fellow students and faculty turned it into a remarkable success!

Today • Growing Strong

400+ Happy Homes Across Pakistan

Alhamdulillah, through dispatched courier deliveries nationwide and university pop-ups, we have handcrafted and sent out more than 400 unique creations. We are still a proud student-led startup, and the warmth of our community keeps inspiring us to dream bigger every day. 🌸
